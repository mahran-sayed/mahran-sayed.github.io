## Overview

A CanSat is a satellite the size of a soft-drink can, dropped from a rocket or a balloon and recovered by parachute. This one was built in 2021 as part of a training and competition organised by the Space Systems Technology Laboratory ([SSTLab](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sst-lab)) at Cairo University.

## My role

I was responsible for **control and data measurement**.

- Used an Arduino as the microcontroller.
- Wrote the libraries for the sensors listed below.
- Worked with my colleagues on manufacturing the PCBs and the satellite bus.

| Sensor | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| DHT11 | Temperature and humidity |
| BMP180 | Barometric pressure, temperature and altitude |
| u-blox NEO-6M | GPS position |
| MPU6050 | 3-axis accelerometer and 3-axis gyroscope |
