## Overview

**Awesome Aerospace Engineering** is an open-source, curated list of learning resources for aerospace engineering, maintained on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 licence. It has collected hundreds of stars from students and engineers.

## What is inside

- **Core subjects:** fluid mechanics, aerodynamics, CFD, orbital mechanics, rocket propulsion and more.
- **General engineering subjects:** mathematics, engineering mechanics, physics, electrical engineering.
- **General skills:** LaTeX, Excel and coding.
- **Projects:** CanSat, CubeSat, rocketry, rovers and UAVs.
- **Hard skills:** electronics and digital fabrication.
- **Also:** software, podcasts, competitions, communities and documentary films.

## Contribute

The list grows through pull requests. If you know a great course, book or tool that is missing, open a PR on [GitHub](https://github.com/mahran-sayed/awesome-aerospace-engineering).
