## Overview

Since May 2025 I have been a Hydrodynamics and CFD Engineer at MCS Oil. The work centres on underwater vehicles, thrusters and surface vessels. This page summarises the topics I handle; details of individual projects are confidential.

## Topics

### Thrusters
Selection, design, CAD modelling and simulation of thrusters and propellers, including work on the in-house thruster code.

### Geometry and meshing
Geometry preparation and simplification, followed by high-quality meshes. I work with both structured and unstructured approaches: tetrahedral, polyhedral, hexcore and poly-hexcore meshes, chosen to match the physics and the cost budget of each case.

### Hydrofoils
Simulation of hydrofoil lift and drag behaviour.

### Hand calculations and low-fidelity tools
Back-of-the-envelope drag estimates and lower-fidelity tools for conceptual design, which also act as a sanity check for later CFD results. See the blog post on [drag estimation](#/blog/drag-estimation-workflow).

### Post-processing and data analysis
Turning raw CFD fields into numbers a designer can use: forces, coefficients, convergence checks and comparison against estimates.

### Seakeeping
RAO (Response Amplitude Operator) calculations for surface vessels.

### Multiphase flows
Free-surface simulations to obtain hull resistance.

### Angular drag
Rotating-arm test simulations to obtain the angular (rotational) drag of vehicles.

### DEM
Discrete Element Method simulations for granular and particulate problems.
