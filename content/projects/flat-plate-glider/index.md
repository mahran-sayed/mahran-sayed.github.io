## Overview

An aerodynamics project (2024) on a **flat plate glider**. A flat plate is the simplest possible wing: no camber and almost no thickness. At the low Reynolds numbers of small gliders it behaves surprisingly well, which makes it a good case study for how lift, drag and glide ratio depend on angle of attack.
