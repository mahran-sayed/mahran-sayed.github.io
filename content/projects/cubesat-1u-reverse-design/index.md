## Overview

Reverse structural design of the **Artemis 1U CubeSat** from the University of Hawaii (2025). Reverse design means starting from an existing, flight-proven satellite and rebuilding its structure as a clean, documented design, to understand why each feature is there.

A 1U CubeSat is a 10 cm cube, so every millimetre of the structure has to carry load, hold the boards and leave room for the deployer rails.
